How to Play

Hazardous chemicals are swarming. Stay compliant. Stay alive.

1 · Move

QWE ASD

Use W A S D to run through the laboratory maze. Keep moving — the hazards never stop.

2 · Aim & Fire

Aim with the mouse, then hold left-click to fire your compliance blaster. Release to stop.

3 · Power Up With Helix

Grab the floating Helix icons. Each one pauses the action and tells you what it does:

4 · Know Your Hazards

Every enemy is a hazardous chemical wearing its GHS pictogram — SVHCs, PFAS, endocrine disruptors and worse. Each hit drains your Compliance Meter. At zero… ECHA consumes you.

5 · Bank Your Score

ESC

Score grows every second you survive, plus a bonus per hazard neutralised. Press Esc to pause — you can exit and bank your score to the leaderboard at any time.
